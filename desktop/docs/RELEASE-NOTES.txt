MARKETAI 1.1.0 — ACCOUNT SYNC AND EVIDENCE RELEASE

What changed:
- Forecast projects 7, 30 and 60 days using the actual collection timestamps.
- Missing/invalid observations are excluded without changing their date alignment.
- Limited history is labeled preliminary; projections are not guaranteed prices.
- Recommendation evidence highlights collection dates, listing counts, cost floors,
  margin assumptions, incompatible listings and missing market evidence.
- A guided flow connects product analysis, margin comparison and monitoring.
- Analyses are saved in account-scoped Cloud history and can be reopened across devices.
- Legacy local history is imported once as labeled summaries, not verified evidence.
- Monitored products use the Cloud Sentinel list, with manual refresh, local caches
  and explicit feedback when saving or synchronization fails.
- Copilot addresses margin, costs, alerts and forecasts using saved account context.
- Users can select a monitored product for Copilot and Forecast.
- Deterministic Copilot fallback works without an AI provider in all five languages.
- Signing out clears the analysis and account-specific content displayed by the UI.
- README, development instructions and privacy notice describe the new workflow.

Validation:
- 33 Cloud tests and 17 Desktop tests passed.
- Browser regression checks two devices, five languages, migration, evidence,
  Forecast, Copilot, offline cache behavior, deletion and logout isolation.
- Browser market data is synthetic test data, not a live marketplace validation.

Deployment:
- Update Cloud and Desktop together to version 1.1.0.
- Cloud startup creates the additional analysis_history table.
- Sentinel collection requires its worker to remain running.
- External providers still require the operator's own credentials and quotas.

Community foundation:
- Free and open source; no paid plans or monthly MarketAI quota.
- English, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Español and Français.
- MarketAI never fabricates market prices when evidence is insufficient.
